# Description (300 words)
description: |
  This dataset accompanies the working paper "The WKB Geometry of Newton
  Polygons: A Classification Theory". It collects the complete computational
  atlas, structural invariants, conjectural laws, and theorem–proof skeletons
  produced over five experimental phases of a fleet-of-agents investigation
  into the classification of scalar linear operators with one irregular
  singularity.

  The central object is the functor F : L ↦ C(L) sending a scalar operator L
  (ODE, difference, or q-difference, with polynomial coefficients and one
  irregular singularity at infinity) to its WKB constellation
  C(L) = Roots(χ), where χ(c) is the WKB characteristic polynomial.

  The atlas covers 96 operators (48 single-edge + 48 multi-edge / systems /
  resonant / nested) across slopes p/q with q up to 7, ranks r up to 3, and
  ODE / difference / q-difference settings. For every operator we record the
  Newton polygon, the inner factorisation χ(c) = χ_w(c^q), the ring
  decomposition C(L) = ⊔_w {c : c^q = w}, the Δ_ij geometry (modulus / argument
  partitions), the Galois / nested-ramification structure, and a 23-class
  taxonomy of single-edge constellations.

  Phase IV lifts the discrete invariants into the analytic layer (Levelt–
  Turrittin formal classification, anti-Stokes skeleton on the ramified cover,
  wild character variety transversality). Phase V consolidates the resulting
  structural laws into eight theorem–proof pairs (M1, N1, D2, S1, AT1, AT3,
  AT5, AT7), each guarded by an 11-stratum counterexample knowledge base.

  Deliverables include: full source pipelines (fleet.py … fleet5.py), JSON
  datasets, narrative phase reports, the paper draft, and the constellation
  zoo figure. The package is intended both as a reproducibility artefact and
  as a citable companion to the forthcoming paper.

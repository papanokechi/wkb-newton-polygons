Computational atlas
-------------------
* 48 single-edge operators (slopes 1/2, 1/3, 1/4, 2/3, 3/2; valuations
  simple / double / mixed; ranks 1, 2, 3; ODE / difference / q-difference).
* 48 Phase-III operators extending the zoo to:
    - multi-edge Newton polygons (MULTI_*, MULTI3_*),
    - block-diagonal systems (SYS2_*, SYS3_*),
    - resonant slopes p/q with q up to 7 (HIGHQ_*),
    - nested ramification (NEST_*),
    - controlled Δ_ij degeneracies (CTRL_*, MIX_*),
    - p-visibility families (PINV_*).
* Newton polygons (vertices, slopes, valuation patterns) for every operator.
* Characteristic polynomials χ(c) and inner polynomials χ_w(w).
* Computed exponent constellations C(L) with ring decomposition, radii,
  angular spacing, and global phase.

-----------------------
* The 23-class single-edge taxonomy (constellations.png + clusters.json).
* The 39-cluster multi-axis universality classification (phase3_clusters.json).
* Δ-modulus partitions, Δ-argument partitions, ω-resonance flags, Galois orbits,
  nested ramification depth, multi-edge interference patterns.

Analytic lifting (Phase IV)
---------------------------
* Levelt–Turrittin / Stokes-matrix / wild-character-variety framework synthesis.
* Mapping table from discrete invariants to analytic invariants.
* Anti-Stokes skeleton (rays, multiplicities) for 20 sampled operators.
* Cover-to-base projection data and Δ-argument collision strata.
* Consistency report: which discrete invariants survive analytically.

Formal mathematical content
---------------------------
* Ten Phase-III conjectures: M1, N1, D1, D2, O1, S1, P1, F1, G1
  (note: M2 was absorbed into M1 in the corrected Phase-V form).
* Eight Phase-IV theorem candidates AT1–AT8.

    M1   — per-edge factorisation (multi-edge generalisation),
    N1   — nested ramification (iterated μ_q-pullback),
    D2   — inner-polynomial Z-resonance dichotomy (prime q),

    AT5  — multi-edge slope filtration on the anti-Stokes skeleton,

2.  Summary of what is NOT YET INCLUDED
================================================================================

* Explicit numerical Stokes matrices for the sampled operators.
  Phase IV records anti-Stokes RAY POSITIONS (the skeleton) and verifies the
  combinatorial cover-vs-base predictions; the next layer — explicit
  Stokes-constant values and their gluing — is deferred to v3.0.
* Monodromy representations, wild character variety coordinates as functions
  on a moduli space, and irregular Riemann–Hilbert data.
* Camera-ready LaTeX sources for the paper. v1.0 ships the draft as a single
  Markdown file in /paper-draft; v2.0 will provide the structured LaTeX
  build.
* Proof of the four Phase-IV theorems NOT promoted in Phase V — AT2 (Stokes
  multiplier degeneracy on the discriminant locus), AT4 (Galois-orbit
  collapse under analytic equivalence), AT6 (resonance-driven inflation of
  the discrete part of MW), and AT8 (system-level analytic factorisation).
  These remain as theorem candidates in phase4_theorems.json.
* Operators with multiple irregular singularities. The entire framework is
  set at a single irregular singularity at infinity; the multi-singular-point
  problem (global isomonodromy) is out of scope for v1.0–v3.0 of this
  dataset.
* q-difference Stokes analogues. The framework is sketched in §8 of the
  paper draft but the analytic lifting is restricted to the ODE setting for
  AT1, AT3, AT5, AT7. Birkhoff–Trjitzinsky (difference) and Ramis-θ-Stokes
  (q-difference) lifts are planned for a separate companion release.


================================================================================
3.  Known limitations
================================================================================

(a) **Open-stratum hypotheses (OS1)–(OS5)** are required for the analytic
    theorems AT1, AT3, AT5, AT7. On the complement (degenerate strata), the
    counterexamples in counterexamples.json document the failure modes; the
    correct statements on these strata are open.

(b) **D2 is stated for prime q.** The composite-q case requires the sub-cyclotomic
    sub-lattice structure of Z[ζ_q] and is not fully treated; a sketched
    extension is included as a remark in the proof.

(c) **AT3 reframing.** The Phase-IV draft of AT3 incorrectly stated that
    nested factorisation increases the minimal Levelt–Turrittin cover from q
    to q·q'. The Phase-V version corrects this: the minimal cover is still q,
    and nested factorisation imposes a μ_q' symmetry on the wild data (NOT
    an analytic ramification refinement). The Phase-IV ray-count formula
    2 p q' per pair is replaced by 2p per pair, in agreement with AT1. The
    correction note is preserved in proofs.md for transparency.

(d) **Phase-III conjecture P1** holds at the level of the constellation but
    fails analytically: p is visible in the cover-ray pattern (Theorem AT7).
    This is documented as a deliberate refinement, not a contradiction; see
    AT7 §Part (4).

(e) **System pullback S1** is stated for block-diagonal systems only.
    Genuinely coupled systems with non-diagonalisable leading symbol lie
    outside the scope of v1.0; partial coverage for systems with
    diagonalisable, distinct-eigenvalue leading symbol is sketched in the
    proof remark.

(f) **Counterexample knowledge base.** The 11 strata in
    counterexamples.json are not claimed to be exhaustive; they are the
    minimal set required to harden the eight Phase-V theorems against the
    operators present in the atlas. Additional strata may appear once the
    multi-singular-point or genuinely-coupled-system cases are studied.

(g) **Reproducibility caveat.** All Phase-I–V computations use exact
    symbolic / rational arithmetic and are bytewise reproducible. The
    rubber-duck critique passes used during Phase V are not encoded in the
    pipeline; the proofs in proofs.md reflect the post-critique state.

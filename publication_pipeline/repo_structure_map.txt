================================================================================
Mirror property
================================================================================

The top-level directories paper/, arxiv_bundle/, journal_bundle/, data/,
reports/, figures/, code/, fleets/ mirror the workspace exactly. The
only additions at the repo root are .gitignore, README.md, CITATION.cff,
CONTRIBUTING.md, CHANGELOG.md, LICENSE-CODE, LICENSE-TEXT.

The major_revision/ and publication_pipeline/ directories are committed
for transparency (revision history and release workflow) but are not
part of the canonical "compile the paper" or "use the atlas" surface.

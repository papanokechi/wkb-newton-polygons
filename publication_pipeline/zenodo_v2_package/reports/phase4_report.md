# Phase IV - Analytic Lifting of the WKB / Newton-Polygon Classification

## 1. Classical analytic framework (analytic_reader)

**Scope.** Phase IV restricts the analytic lift to scalar linear ODEs at an irregular singularity x = infinity. Difference (Birkhoff-Trjitzinsky) and q-difference (Ramis) Stokes phenomena have analogous structures but require separate frameworks; they are documented but not analysed here.

**Levelt Turrittin.** For a meromorphic connection at x = infinity with one irregular singularity and Katz invariant p/q in lowest terms, after pulling back to the ramified cover x = t^q the formal connection splits as a direct sum of rank-one summands whose irregular types are polynomials Q_i(t) in C[t] of degree <= p. The leading coefficient c_i of Q_i is the i-th root of the WKB characteristic polynomial chi(c) from Phase I.

**Irregular Type.** On the cover t-plane, each formal solution carries a factor exp(Q_i(t)) times a formal monodromy factor t^{nu_i}. The collection (Q_i, nu_i) is the *irregular type* and forms the discrete part of the wild character variety.

**Anti Stokes Directions.** For an ordered pair (i, j) of formal exponentials, the anti-Stokes direction is the set of arguments phi of t at which Re(Q_i(t) - Q_j(t)) = 0 to leading order. Writing Q_i - Q_j = L_{ij} t^{d_{ij}} + lower order, where L_{ij} is the leading coefficient and d_{ij} = deg(Q_i - Q_j) <= p, the anti-Stokes directions in the t-plane satisfy arg(L_{ij}) + d_{ij} * phi = pi/2 (mod pi), yielding 2 d_{ij} directions in [0, 2 pi). In the generic case d_{ij} = p and L_{ij} = c_i - c_j = Delta_{ij}; in degenerate cases d_{ij} < p (leading terms cancel).

**Stokes Sectors.** The complement of the union of anti-Stokes directions in the t-plane is a disjoint union of open sectors. On each sector S, there is a unique multi-summation lifting the formal solution to a holomorphic one, and the dominance order Re(Q_i(t)) > Re(Q_j(t)) is constant on S.

**Stokes Groups.** Stokes multipliers between adjacent sectors form unipotent matrices whose support (nonzero off-diagonal entries) is constrained by the dominance order across the shared anti-Stokes direction: entry (i, j) is allowed only when Q_j dominates Q_i on the *positive* side of the direction. The Stokes group at a given anti-Stokes direction is the unipotent subgroup of GL_N(C) generated by all such allowed entries.

**Wild Character Variety.** The wild character variety MW(L) parametrises {formal monodromy} x {Stokes data} / {gauge}. As an algebraic variety its dimension equals the irregularity of L; its discrete strata are indexed by (i) the multiset of leading coefficients {c_i} (= the constellation C(L)) and (ii) finer invariants of the irregular type (lower-order Q_i coefficients, formal monodromy).

**Difference Q Difference Remark.** For difference operators, Stokes phenomena occur in vertical strips and are organised by the Birkhoff connection coefficients; for q-difference operators, Ramis's theta-Stokes structure replaces sectors with annular regions. The constellation C(L) and Delta_{ij} data continue to make sense in both contexts (they are combinatorial), but the anti-Stokes-direction picture does NOT translate verbatim. We document this gap and treat scalar ODEs only in the lifting.

## 7. How Phase IV extends the classification

Phase I-III produced a fully combinatorial / arithmetic classification: F : (local model L) -> (multiset {c_i}, Delta_{ij} partitions, resonance signatures, Galois orbits). Phase IV lifts this picture into the analytic Stokes layer WITHOUT computing global Stokes constants (which are operator-specific transcendentals). What we DO obtain is the *anti-Stokes skeleton*: the combinatorial Stokes structure determined by leading-order data.

**The lift is faithful on:**

- *Anti-Stokes ray positions* on the cover (T1 / AT1) - exact lift of D1.
- *Cover refinement* under nested ramification (AT3) - structural lift.
- *Direct-sum decomposition* for genuine systems (AT4).
- *Slope filtration* on the Stokes structure for multi-edge polygons (AT5).
- *Galois-equivariant strata* of the discrete part of the wild character variety (AT6).

**The lift detects but does not refine (necessary, not sufficient):**

- *omega-resonance -> Stokes-group enhancement* (AT2): cluster enhancement is ALWAYS present (max_mult>=2 in every Phase-III omega-resonant operator), but the naive prediction multiplicity = m/q is sub-naive at leading order. Full m/q enhancement requires alignment of lower-order Q_i coefficients (higher-codimension stratum).
- *Stokes constants* (numerical entries of Stokes matrices): global transcendental invariants; Phase IV only specifies their *support pattern* (which off-diagonal entries can be nonzero).
- *Lower-order Q_i terms*: needed to upgrade leading-order symmetry inflation (C_n with n > q) to a full automorphism of MW(L); only the leading symmetry is captured.

**Negative results:**

- *p-invariance (P1)* is FALSE for analytic Stokes data (AT7): the slope numerator p is invisible at the Newton-polygon / Delta-partition level but visible in the anti-Stokes ray positions through gcd(2p, q) projection identifications.
- *chi_w-strata (D2)* are INVISIBLE to the local Stokes skeleton (AT8): they show up as algebraic constraints on the position of MW(L) in its ambient moduli, not as local Stokes structure.

**Out of scope:** Difference and q-difference operators (their analytic Stokes structures are organised differently; the Phase-III invariants make combinatorial sense in those settings but the anti-Stokes-direction picture does not transfer verbatim). Coupled systems (non-block-diagonal) are also out of scope; only direct-sum systems (S1 / AT4) are treated.

**Net effect:** F factors as a tower of refinements where each layer adds analytic content: Newton polygon -> chi(c) -> {c_i} -> Delta_{ij} -> arg(Delta_{ij}) -> anti-Stokes skeleton -> (full Stokes matrices: out of scope here). Phase IV pushes the classification one full layer beyond Phase III into analysis, while stopping short of the global Stokes constants.
